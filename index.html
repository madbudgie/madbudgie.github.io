Our game

Courier Guy

A stylized courier and vehicle game built around riding, deliveries, upgrades and the everyday chaos of a city inspired by Istanbul.

Take delivery jobs, explore the city, earn money, tune your ride and build your own courier routine. Courier Guy mixes arcade-friendly driving with a colorful low-poly world.

About Mad Budgie

Small by design.

Mad Budgie Games is an independent game development label — not a giant studio pretending to be one.

The focus is simple: build games with a recognizable personality, a strong visual identity and systems that are fun to mess around with. Courier Guy is the first big step in that direction.
